About JITA 2025

The Junior conference on Informatics: Theory and Applications

JITA 2025 is the 2nd edition of the Junior conference on Informatics: Theory and Applications, a multidisciplinary conference that allows Masters and PhD students in various fields of Theoretical and Applied Computer Science to present their research results. Take the opportunity to share your research with colleagues, all the while honing your presentation skills, receiving feedback, and broadening your knowledge of Computer Science. Any Masters or PhD Student is welcome to present either their current research or ongoing works with preliminary results.

Topics of Interest

Submissions are encouraged in areas including (but not limited to): Algorithms and Complexity, Bioinformatics, Centralized and Distributed algorithms, Cryptography and Security, Quantum Computing, Graph theory, Human-Computer Interaction, Modeling and Simulation, Optimization, Virtual and Augmented Reality, Verification and Formal methods.

Keynote Speakers

Combinatorial Optimization in real-world production systems Supply Chain Management
Prof. Chengbin CHU
Professor, Gustave Eiffel University
This talk covers supply chain management fundamentals through combinatorial optimization techniques and current research topics. The focus is on real-world applications across diverse sectors, from multinational corporations to small-and-medium enterprises, demonstrating practical implementation of research findings.
Algorithms for #SAT: A survey presenting different ways of counting the models of a CNF formula
Prof. Florent Capelli
Junior Professor, Université d'Artois
A comprehensive survey of different approaches to counting models of CNF formulas (the standard input of SAT solvers) and how their computational complexity relates to the graph structure of the input CNF.
