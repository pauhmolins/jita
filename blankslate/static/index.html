The Junior Conference on Informatics: Theory and Applications (JITA) is a great opportunity for master students and young researchers to present recent research results. Receiving feedback and sharing your initial research results presents a chance to expand your knowledge and presentation skills. Keynote presentations by researchers from academia and industry are included for you to start building your professional network.

This event is co-organised by Université Paris-Saclay (UPSaclay) and Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IPP).
